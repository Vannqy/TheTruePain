PSX Motel Pickup Icons v1.0
by AssetsFactory

32 free inventory icons of small props from a run-down 1990s American roadside motel.
Each icon is rendered from one of our free 3D models, so the item the player picks up in the
world looks exactly like its icon in the inventory.

FOLDERS
  512/ 256/ 128/ 64/   detailed icons, PNG with transparent background and a thin dark outline
  ps1_64/              PS1-style icons: rendered at 64 px without anti-aliasing, 15-bit colour
                       with ordered dithering, 1-bit transparency
  ps1_128/             the same PS1 icons enlarged x2 with nearest neighbour (crisp pixels)
  atlas/               one sheet per folder, with a CSV of positions (name,x,y,width,height)

Tip: for the PS1 icons, set texture filtering to Nearest / Point in your engine and scale by whole numbers.
